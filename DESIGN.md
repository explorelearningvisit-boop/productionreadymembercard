# BAD GYM — Current product design direction

**Product.** A phone-first gym member dashboard for check-in, attendance, workouts, payment and body progress.  
**Character.** Athletic, assured, human and precise.  
**World.** Deep forest-emerald frame around a bright pearl-white glass workspace.  
**Moments.** Ice-glass Freeze, tangible fitness product art in four KPI cards, attendance ring/calendar.  
**Signature.** Compact Member Intelligence with a deep-emerald contextual rail and pearl data panel.

Emerald + pearl is the app language. Purple is not shell/rail/frame/background. Gold=Gold Plan; ice=Freeze; sky=Payment; muted lavender=Workout; mint=Attendance/Body. Rose=due/absent, amber=late, muted violet=holiday, grey=off. Keep pearl dominant, restrained depth, no screen glow/rainbow gradients/gradient text/heavy blur. Preserve order and 360dp one-screen composition. Verify 320/360/390dp and 560–800dp usable heights. Keep four KPI modules and six-item rail. Use transparent PulseVault art with BoxFit.contain; text/data stay Flutter. No fake LIVE/transactions/partners/AI/health claims. Flutter/Dart native; finite motion and reduced-motion. Old purple documents are historical where conflicting.
